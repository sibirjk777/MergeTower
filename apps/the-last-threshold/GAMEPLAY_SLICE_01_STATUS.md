# VERTICAL SLICE 01 — STATUS

Статус: playable prototype implemented.

## Core

- Platformer-first gameplay.
- Running and jumping.
- Double jump unlocked by the first interaction mini-game.
- Coyote time and jump buffer.
- Basic melee combat.
- Enemy patrol and damage.
- Checkpoint altar.
- Three memory shards.
- Final gate interaction.
- Adaptive camera for mobile/desktop.
- Large touch controls.

## Interactive loop

1. Find Mira's bag.
2. Obtain the Book of Threshold.
3. Open the book.
4. Reach the seal mechanism.
5. Play «Слияние Печатей».
6. Obtain Double Jump.
7. Traverse the upper route.
8. Fight the Memory Guardian.
9. Collect memory shards.
10. Bind to the altar.
11. Reach the final gate.
12. Receive Mira's cliffhanger message.
