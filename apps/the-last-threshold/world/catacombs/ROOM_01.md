# Catacombs — Room 01

## Gameplay beat
The player enters after seeing Irys. The corridor is intentionally tight and quiet.

1. Stone pressure plate triggers a trap.
2. A creature emerges from an alcove.
3. The player can evade it rather than fight immediately.
4. A Memory Shard is found near a broken altar.
5. The shard becomes the first persistent collectible in the catacombs.
6. The room ends at a locked descent gate.

## Design intent
The first catacomb room teaches danger, recovery, exploration and resource persistence without turning into a combat tutorial arena.
